## Notes

- **Total: 19 businesses** — 10 recent (Rounds 3–4) + 9 earlier (Rounds 1–2).
- Most sites run on free `wixsite.com` subdomains; a few have custom domains (Electric Love, Artful Cake Studio, 2OldCrows, Chef Gina's, Kern, Hair Tech Design).
- Well-built custom-domain Wix sites usually turned out too visible (70–470 reviews, top rankings) — genuinely modern + low-visibility + custom domain is a rare combination.
- No plumber passed the modern bar in Rounds 3–4.
